Easy money words

Treasury bill (T-bill): you lend money to the government for a short time, and they pay you back with extra.

Fixed deposit: you leave money in a bank for a set time, and the bank pays you extra.

Mutual fund: many people put money together, and experts invest it for them.

Stocks: you buy a small piece of a company. Its value can go up or down.

Compound growth: your growth also grows. Like a snowball rolling down a hill, it gets bigger faster over time.

Stay safe: only invest with companies licensed by the SEC Ghana or the Bank of Ghana. If someone promises very big money very fast, it is probably a scam.
