Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to review businesses and keep the community safe. Your ID card is locked away, only Selorm can see it, and it is never shown on the website. We never sell your information.

Who we are

Grow With Selorm is a free website run by Selorm Yao Junior Ahiabor in Ghana. It helps small business owners get seen by people who may want to invest. You can contact us using the email on our Contact section.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. Your password is scrambled (encrypted), so nobody can read it, not even Selorm.
  • Your business details: business name, type, town, story, sales, how much money you need, what an investor gets, and your phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Your ID: the name on your Ghana Card and a photo or PDF of your Ghana Card or other ID.
  • Reports you send us about problems or scams.
  • Simple visit information: which pages are opened, whether a phone or computer is used, which website or app the visitor came from (for example WhatsApp or Google), and a random visitor code saved in your browser. We do not save your IP address in this record, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

  • To let you log in and manage your businesses.
  • To review businesses before they are shown, and to check that owners are real people.
  • To protect members from fake businesses and scams.
  • To understand how many people use the site, so we can make it better.

Who can see your information

  • Everyone can see an approved business: its name, type, town, story, sales, amount needed, what the investor gets, and the owner's name. Your phone number is only shown if you ticked the box to allow it.
  • Only you and Selorm can see your ID card, the name on your Ghana Card, your email address, and businesses that are not approved.
  • Only Selorm can see reports and visit information.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Where it is kept

Accounts, businesses, ID cards and visit information are stored with Supabase, a secure online database, on servers in the European Union (Ireland). The website itself is hosted by GitHub Pages. Some emails are sent to Selorm through FormSubmit to tell him about new members, businesses and reports. These companies may keep short technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep their services safe.

How long we keep it

  • Your business and ID card are kept until you delete the business, or until you ask us to delete them. When a business is deleted, its ID card file is deleted too.
  • Visit information is used for about 30 days on the dashboard and may be cleared after that.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. You can delete your own businesses from My account. For anything else, contact us and we will reply within 2 days. You also have rights under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and you can contact the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Age

You must be 18 or older to make an account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
